About this set

These outcomes come from the John McCoy (lulilaš) Since Time Immemorial: Tribal Sovereignty in Washington State curriculum, published by the OSPI Office of Native Education in partnership with Washington’s federally recognized tribes and licensed CC BY 4.0. Teaching it is required in all Washington common schools under RCW 28A.320.170. The curriculum itself lives at OSPI and is free.

The Big Five are the outcomes OSPI sets for the end of middle school. The five Essential Questions frame inquiry across a unit rather than grading a single task, so treat them as unit level.

The four level descriptors below are local wording, not OSPI’s. STI is place based by design, so the strongest version of these names the tribes nearest our schools. Talk with your building or district STI lead before using them to grade.

STI.MS.1Big Five

Understanding why treaty rights outrank state law

Official standard text

Understand that, according to the U.S. Constitution, treaties are "the supreme law of the land"; consequently treaty rights supersede most state laws.
