H1.6-8.6History

Explaining what makes an era an era

Official standard text

Explain how themes and developments help to define eras in United States history from 1763 to 1877, including: Fighting for independence (1763-1783); Establishing the new nation (1781-1815); Slavery, expansion, removal, and reform (1801-1850); Civil War and Reconstruction (1850-1877)

H1: Understands historical chronology.

C1.6-8.3Civics

Explaining America's founding ideals

Official standard text

Explain key ideals and principles outlined in the Declaration of Independence, including life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness; the U.S. Constitution, including the rule of law, separation of powers, representative government, and popular sovereignty; and, the Bill of Rights, including due process and freedom of expression.

C1: Understands key ideals and principles of the United States, including those in the Declaration of Independence, Constitution, and other foundational documents.
