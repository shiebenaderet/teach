H1.6-8.5History

Explaining what makes an era in Washington history

Official standard text

Explain how themes and developments have defined eras in Washington state history from 1854 to the present: Territory and treaty-making (1854-1889); Railroads, reform, immigration, and labor (1889-1930); Turmoil and triumph (1930-1974); New technologies and industries in contemporary Washington (1975-present)

H1: Understands historical chronology.
